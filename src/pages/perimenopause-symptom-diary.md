# A perimenopause symptom diary for irregular months

Perimenopause rarely follows a pattern you can remember in a ten-minute appointment. A diary helps you show what actually happened, week by week.

## A check-in that takes seconds
Rate the day from 1 to 5 and tap the symptoms that came, each on a short scale. The list starts with the ones people mention most — night sweats, trouble sleeping, brain fog, low mood, irritability, joint pain — and you can add your own.

Nothing is required. A day you don't log stays blank; it is never counted as "no symptoms". There are no streaks and no reminders about days you missed, and past days can be filled in whenever you like.

## Periods, however they come
Mark a period when it starts and when it ends. Tansy never asks for a last-period date and never predicts the next one: a long gap is simply shown as a fact.

## HRT and dose changes
Add your HRT — patch, gel, spray, tablet or vaginal — and record each dose change on its date. The calendar and charts carry those dates, so you can look at the weeks before and after a change.
