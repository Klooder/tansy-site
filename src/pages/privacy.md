# Tansy Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026. Tansy is developed by Ivan Borovets, sole proprietor (FOP), Kyiv, Ukraine ("we").

**In one sentence: everything you record in Tansy stays on your iPhone, and we receive none of it.**

## What Tansy keeps, and where
Your diary — hot flashes, symptoms, periods, HRT and other medications, notes, the answers you confirm for the symptom scale, and your settings — is stored only on your device. Backups of your device that you make yourself (for example iCloud Backup) can include it under Apple's terms; we cannot open them.

## Apple Health
Tansy uses Apple Health only if you turn it on in Settings, and only for the types you allow.
- **Reading:** sleep, awakenings, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and wrist temperature. Tansy keeps daily summaries of these values in a separate store on your device so they can be shown next to your hot flashes. That store is never synced or sent anywhere.
- **Writing:** if you allow it, each hot flash or night sweat you log is saved to Health on your device.
- Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your device through Tansy. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

## What we collect
Nothing. Tansy has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no server.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple. Apple tells the app only whether you have Tansy Pro; we never receive payment details.

## Reminders
The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

## Your control
Settings → Export creates CSV files of everything you have recorded, at any time and for free. Deleting a past report's answers is possible in Settings → Report history. Deleting the app deletes the data it stores on the device.

## Children
Tansy is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
The current version of this policy is always at https://klooder.github.io/tansy-site/privacy/ with its effective date.
