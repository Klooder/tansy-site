# A menopause symptom checklist to take to your doctor

The Greene Climacteric Scale is a short questionnaire used in menopause research and clinics. It lists 21 symptoms, and for each you mark how much it bothers you at the moment: not at all, a little, quite a bit or extremely.

## Download it free
[Greene Climacteric Scale (PDF, one page)](greene-climacteric-scale.pdf) — blank, ready to print and fill in by hand. The wording is the scale's own: Greene JG, Constructing a standard climacteric scale, Maturitas 1998;29:25-31, as published in the PhenX Toolkit.

## What the scale covers
- **Psychological** (items 1–11): heartbeat, tension, sleep, excitability, panic, concentration, tiredness, interest, mood, crying, irritability.
- **Somatic** (items 12–18): dizziness, pressure in the head or body, numbness, headaches, muscle and joint pains, loss of feeling, breathing.
- **Vasomotor** (items 19–20): hot flushes and sweating at night.
- **Sexuality** (item 21): loss of interest in sex.

The checklist describes how you feel; it does not diagnose anything. Your clinician decides what it means for you.
