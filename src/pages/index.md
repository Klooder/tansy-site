# Tansy

A diary for perimenopause and menopause, for the years when your body changes faster than your appointments come round.

- **One tap per hot flash.** Tansy notes the time, so the nights and the hours they gather around show up on their own.
- **A daily check-in in seconds.** Rate the day, tap the symptoms that came. Nothing is required, and a skipped day stays blank.
- **Periods, HRT and dose changes**, with their dates on the calendar and the charts.
- **A PDF for your doctor** with the Greene Climacteric Scale, filled in from your diary and checked by you.
- **No account.** Your diary stays on your iPhone; we never see it.

Tansy is free to use. Tansy Pro adds the doctor report, 90-day charts and Apple Health values in the charts.
